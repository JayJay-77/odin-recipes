Ultimate Bacon-Wrapped Cheese Chicken

This recipe wraps tender chicken in crispy bacon and melts cheese right on top.

Ingredients

  • 1.5 lbs chicken tenderloins
  • 1 slice cheddar cheese per tenderloin
  • 1 pack bacon slices, cut in half
  • Salt and pepper, to taste

How to make

  1. Heat your oven to 425°F (220°C).
  2. Put parchment paper on a baking sheet.
  3. Lay out the chicken tenderloins and season with salt and pepper.
  4. Place one slice of cheese on top of each piece of chicken.
  5. Wrap the bacon tightly around the chicken so the cheese stays facing up.
  6. Bake for 20–25 minutes until the bacon is crispy.

Notes: You can secure the bacon with toothpicks if needed. For extra crispiness, broil for 1–2 minutes at the end—watch closely.
