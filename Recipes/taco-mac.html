Loaded Taco Mac and Cheese

This dish combines creamy macaroni and cheese with savory taco beef.

Ingredients

  • 1 box (14 oz) macaroni and cheese
  • 1 lb ground beef
  • 1 packet taco seasoning
  • 1 can (10 oz) diced tomatoes with green chilies

How to make

  1. Cook the macaroni and cheese following the package directions, then drain.
  2. In a separate pan, cook the ground beef until brown and drain the grease.
  3. Stir the taco seasoning and the canned tomatoes into the cooked beef.
  4. Mix the beef mixture directly into the pot of prepared mac and cheese.
  5. Stir well and serve warm.
