Quick Sweet Orange Chicken Nuggets

Turn frozen chicken nuggets into a sticky, sweet takeout-style meal in minutes.

Ingredients

  • 30 oz frozen chicken nuggets
  • 1 cup orange marmalade
  • 1/2 cup barbecue sauce
  • 1 tablespoon soy sauce

How to make

  1. Bake the chicken nuggets in the oven following the package instructions until hot and crispy.
  2. In a bowl, mix the orange marmalade, barbecue sauce, and soy sauce.
  3. Microwave the sauce for 1 minute until warm and easy to stir.
  4. Toss the cooked chicken nuggets in the sauce until fully coated.
